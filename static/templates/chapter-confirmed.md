A few things worth knowing.

**Keeping the chapter active.** Hold your first event within the next six
months, then keep the chapter active with at least one event every six
months. Many chapters run something every two or three months. If that
frequency turns out to be hard, tell us rather than going quiet - ask in
the organisers' Slack, or sign up at
https://rladies.org/form/mentoring-signup/ and we will pair you with an
experienced organiser. We would much rather help than retire a chapter.

**Publish every event on Meetup.** That is what counts as activity, and
it is how members find you. Events we cannot see on Meetup are events we
cannot count.

**Look after the chapter email.** Use it to create any social accounts,
and cc it on replies so your co-organisers can see them. Keep its
password in a password manager rather than a document or a chat message,
turn on two-factor authentication, and save the recovery codes somewhere
you will still have them if you lose your phone.

**Keep your chapter's details current.** When anything changes - a new
social account, a new website - update your chapter's entry at
https://github.com/rladies/rladies.github.io/tree/main/data/chapters.
That file is what the website and our own tooling read.

**Adding co-organisers.** There is one official route, and it is
described at
https://guide.rladies.org/organization/intro/co-organizers/#how-to-onboard-organizers.
Please use it rather than adding people directly on Meetup.
