RLadies+ runs as a US-registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit out of California, paid for by a mix of one-off donations, recurring sponsors, and grant income from partners like the R-Consortium and Posit.
Money in covers conference travel, the small set of paid tools that keep the org running (Zoom, Calendly, Google Workspace, Meetup), reimbursements to chapter organisers for venue and refreshment costs, and the occasional honorarium for invited speakers.

Three external services handle the actual movement of money:

- [Relay]({{% relref "relay" %}}) is the primary USD operating account — the centre of gravity for incoming grant payments and outgoing operational spend.
- [Wise]({{% relref "wise" %}}) handles anything cross-border — paying speakers or chapters abroad, receiving donations denominated in non-USD currencies.
- [PayPal]({{% relref "paypal" %}}) is the donation channel for one-off contributors and small transactions.

State-level filings, tax returns, and the registered-agent service are handled separately through [VCorp]({{% relref "/global-team/nonprofit-administration/vcorp" %}}) in the [nonprofit administration]({{% relref "/global-team/nonprofit-administration" %}}) section.
Credentials for every financial account live in the Shared vault — see [1Password]({{% relref "/global-team/infrastructure/1password" %}}).

## Donations

When someone sends an email inquiring about larger donations, offer to:

- provide them the routing and account number for a direct wire transfer (the account is in Relay; see the Relay page for where the details live in the dashboard), or
- if they are in the US and prefer to send a check, give them the RLadies+ nonprofit HQ address in Oakland — the same address used for official mail (see [AnytimeMailbox]({{% relref "/global-team/nonprofit-administration/anytime-mailbox" %}})).

How to acknowledge the donation:

- Thank the donor in the email communication.
- If the donor is comfortable with a public acknowledgement, add their name to the website's sponsors list.
- If the donor is comfortable with a public acknowledgement, the social media team prepares a draft post for the donor to validate before publishing (see [social media management]({{% relref "/global-team/social-media-management" %}})).

For donations that arrive through PayPal directly, the PayPal page covers receipt issuing and the sweep-to-Relay cadence.

## Recordkeeping

The transaction exports from Relay, Wise, and PayPal are the source of truth for bookkeeping and for the annual Form 990 filing that VCorp prepares.
A periodic export — monthly works well — uploaded into the Shared Drive's finance folder keeps the records reconcilable.
