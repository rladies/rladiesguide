## Template library

This page is the canonical list of RLadies+ Canva templates and the links that let anyone open them.
If you want to make a graphic, start here.

Each link opens the template in Canva and offers **Use template**, which gives you your own editable copy.
You cannot change the original, and nothing you do affects anyone else's copy.

{{% notice info %}}
You need a free Canva account to use a template, and nothing more.
You do not need to be a member of the RLadies+ Canva team, and you do not need a paid plan.
Google Chrome is recommended — Safari has known compatibility issues.
{{% /notice %}}

## Why there is no folder link

Canva folder links (`canva.com/folder/...`) only work for people who are already members of the RLadies+ Canva team.
Everyone else gets an error page, which is why they used to fail for chapter organisers.

Folder links are never shared outside the global team.
Templates are shared one link at a time, and those links live on this page.

## Publishing a template

Anyone maintaining the template library follows these steps.
You must be signed in to the **shared RLadies+ Canva account** — the credentials are in 1Password, as for every other shared account.

{{% notice warning %}}
Do not do this from your personal Canva account, even if you are a member of the RLadies+ team.
Canva makes whoever creates a design its owner, and only the owner can publish or repair its link.
A template owned by an individual stops being fixable the moment that person moves on — this is exactly how the previous set of links broke.
{{% /notice %}}

1. Sign in to Canva as the shared RLadies+ account
2. Open the design and choose **Make a copy** if the design is not already owned by this account — a design marked _View only_ belongs to someone else and its link cannot be published
3. Move the copy into the team-owned templates folder
4. Open the copy, click **Share**
5. Choose **More**, then **Template link**
6. Set the access to anyone with the link, and copy the link
7. Paste the link into the matching row of the tables above
8. Add the template to the [hub page](#finding-templates-visually)
9. Open a pull request with the updated row

{{% notice info %}}
Use the **template link**, not the ordinary share link.
An ordinary share link lets people edit the original in place; a template link gives each person their own copy and leaves the original untouched.
{{% /notice %}}

### Checking a link actually works

A link that works for you proves nothing — you are signed in to the account that owns it.

Open the link in a private or incognito window, signed out of Canva.
If you see the design and a **Use template** button, the link is good.
If you see an error or a sign-in wall, the link is not published and step 6 did not take effect.

## Design ownership

The templates were designed by [Science Graphic Design](https://www.sciencegraphicdesign.com/) as part of the 2026 rebrand.
The agency still owns the original designs, which are shared with the global team as view-only.

The templates linked from this page are RLadies+-owned copies of those originals, made from the shared account so that the community can publish and repair the links without going back to the designers.
If a template needs a design change, the change is made to the RLadies+ copy — the agency originals are kept as the untouched reference.

If you need a layout that does not exist, ask the communications team rather than modifying an existing template — see the [social media management guide]({{% relref "global-team/social-media-management" %}}).
